Broad Match Rater

Rate whether an advertiser keyword and its expansion are a Good, Acceptable, or Bad match.

Step 1 — Enter the Pair

Keyword = the original advertiser keyword.  Expansion = the query it was matched to (the broader term).

Step 2 — Research

Use the App Search Links and Web Search Links in your rating tool to research both the keyword and the expansion individually. Understand what each one is and what users want when they search for it.

🔍 App Search: check both keyword and expansion separately in App Store
🌐 Web Search: look for the keyword and expansion on the web to confirm intent
⚠ If keyword or expansion is in another language, translate it first to understand the intent

Step 3 — Decision Checklist

Check every criterion that applies. Bad overrides everything — if any Bad indicator is checked, the pair is Bad regardless of Good or Acceptable checks.

✓ GOOD — essentially the same query
○ ACCEPTABLE — related, shared intent category
✕ BAD — different intent or different meaning
